The agentic creative canvas
for professional creators.

Plnty is a powerful suite of design tools that turns ideas into finished work across image, video, vector, 3D and audio, all inside one shared canvas.

Book a Demo

A new class of creative tools

Plnty turns frontier models into purpose-built tools with creative intent built in.
Each one gives you a precise new way to find, develop or finish an idea, without having to build the workflow yourself.

Explore all tools

Give Antagonist an image and it pushes back. It finds one assumption in the work, breaks it, and holds everything else in place, returning arguments you can accept or refuse.

Choose what is allowed to change: color, proportion, shape, grade, composition or primitive structure. Variations explores that single axis in eight coherent directions, revealing new possibilities without losing the original idea.

Shape the final image with three-way color controls and 24 browser-native treatments, from print and dither to film, raster and artifact. Immediate, tactile and free to run.

Give any video a new visual language without losing its motion. Add a style reference and Plnty carries its material, color and character through every frame.

Choose a year. Time Machine rebuilds your subject as if it had actually been designed and made then, changing its materials, construction and cultural context, not simply aging the surface.
